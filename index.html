About

A High-Fidelity 3D Extension of How2Sign

We present How2Sign-Synth3D, a large-scale sign language dataset developed through a markerless, holistic performance-capture pipeline. Designed to address the scarcity of high-fidelity 3D motion data in continuous signing, the dataset provides synchronized multi-view recordings, fitted SMPL-H body-model parameters, and video-level transcripts, as well as synthetic renderings with ground-truth annotations to support reproducible research.

Building upon How2Sign, one of the most adopted resources for American Sign Language (ASL) research, How2Sign-Synth3D resolves long-standing limitations such as unsynchronized dual-view recordings, multi-signer interference in panoptic data, and the lack of precise 3D annotations.
Leveraging an iterative fit → render → retrain loop, models trained on How2Sign-Synth3D achieve state-of-the-art accuracy, reducing frontal hand mean landmark error by over 60% relative to the baseline.

Synchronized How2Sign Green Screen Set

Dual-view (frontal and side) recordings temporally aligned using kinematic signatures. We synchronized 89.7% of pairs with an average offset of ~7.7 frames. This data includes fitted SMPL-H parameters, calibrated camera intrinsics/extrinsics, and sentence-level English translations.

🎥

Refined How2Sign Panoptic Subset

Single-signer sequences captured from 7+ cameras in the CMU Panoptic Studio. We created single-signer videos, filtered out flickering views, and optimized camera selection for geometric consistency. This data includes SMPL-H fits, full camera calibration, and video-level translations.

🤖

Synthetic Train Set (100k frames)

High-fidelity renderings generated via Blender's Cycles engine from SMPL-H fits of How2Sign motion. Randomized body shape, clothing, backgrounds, and camera parameters promote generalization. This data provides ground-truth 3D landmarks and SOMA pose/shape parameters.

📊

Synthetic Test Benchmark (50k frames)

Out-of-domain evaluation set derived from an independent Chinese Sign Language motion library, ensuring no overlap in motion priors or signer identities. This data includes a 360° benchmark and a frontal baseline variant for controlled comparisons.

🦴

Dense 3D Landmark Annotations

Hierarchical landmark trackers covering full body (1,428), upper body (1,118), hands (141 per hand), and face (744) landmarks. Probabilistic landmark modeling with uncertainty estimates enables robust multi-view fitting.

✍️

English Translations

Sentence-level translations for the Green Screen set and video-level translations for the Panoptic subset, aligned with corrected timestamps from the synchronization step.
